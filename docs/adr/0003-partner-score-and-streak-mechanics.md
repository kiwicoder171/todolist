# 3. Partner Score, Streak Mechanics, and Penalty Cap

We decided that completing 100% of applicable daily routines earns +1 point and increments the partner's consecutive Streak. Missing 1 day incurs no penalty (grace day); missing 2 consecutive days penalizes -1 point; missing 3 consecutive days penalizes -2 points; and missing 4 or more consecutive days incurs a capped total penalty of -3 points for that absence block. Scores are allowed to drop into negative values to maintain playful yet authentic stakes. Days flagged as Freeze Days pause streak calculations and waive penalties without breaking existing streaks.
